# Limitations

- This is a documentary pre-assessment, not a replacement for expert validation or an in-country participatory assessment.
- Source availability, language, OCR/conversion quality, and documentary gaps can bias what becomes extractable evidence.
- Development and final sources are separated for prompt tuning, but the final Stage 2 assessment intentionally uses the combined evidence bank.
- Model outputs may vary on re-execution even with pinned prompts and low-temperature settings.
- Exact-quote validation establishes textual traceability, not the truth, completeness, relevance, or currentness of a claim.
- Evidence strength and maturity scores contain methodological and model judgment; sensitivity analyses reduce but do not eliminate that uncertainty.
- The archived web pages and PDFs may become stale and may have redistribution restrictions.
- Prompt snapshots make the recorded calls inspectable, but re-execution also requires authorized Langfuse and model-service access.
- The Python requirements are bounded but not fully locked to hashes; the dashboard lockfile is retained.
- Raw low-level request/response dumps were selectively omitted to avoid duplication, credential risk, and an unreviewable repository. The retained canonical manifest and selected experimental artifacts support the handover claims.
