# Negative-source sensitivity results

The frozen Stage 2 P5 prompt (temperature 0) evaluated a blinded bank containing 60 strong genuine records and 155 synthetic low-quality controls. All records used neutral `EV-####` identifiers, and record order was randomized. Control identities were revealed only after scoring.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Genuine records available | 60 |
| Synthetic controls available | 155 |
| Unique controls used | 105 (67.7%) |
| Unique controls used as supporting evidence | 41 |
| Unique controls used as limiting evidence | 69 |
| Control-citation occurrences | 116 |
| Indicators containing at least one control | 27/31 (87.1%) |
| Genuine records used | 52/60 (86.7%) |

## Interpretation

Stage 2 cited 105 of the 155 controls. Most control citations were used to characterize missing or insufficient evidence, but 41 controls were placed in the supporting-evidence field. Of the supporting-control citation occurrences, 38 supported Phase 1 assignments and 7 appeared in assessments above Phase 1. This indicates that the pipeline frequently treated topically matched but non-probative records as usable evidence, particularly as evidence of limitation or documentary absence.

The experimental phase distribution was 18 indicators at Phase 1 and 13 at Phase 2. No indicator received Phase 3 or above. These phase changes must not be attributed solely to synthetic controls because the experimental bank also excluded the development evidence bank and 172 of the 232 genuine evaluation records. A matched 60-strong bank without controls is required to isolate the causal effect of control injection on phase scores.
