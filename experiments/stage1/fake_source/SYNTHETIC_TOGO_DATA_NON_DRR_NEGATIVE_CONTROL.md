# Notes from the Togo Coastal Arts Weekend

The Togo Coastal Arts Weekend took place in Lome over three evenings. The programme included live music, pottery demonstrations, a photography exhibition, and food stalls operated by local vendors. Visitors could buy a day ticket or a weekend pass at the entrance.

For routine bookkeeping, the ticket desk entered the number of tickets sold into a spreadsheet at the close of each evening. The spreadsheet also contained the date, ticket category, and total cash received. These data were used only to reconcile the ticket box with the cash deposit.

The organisers asked visitors to complete a short satisfaction card before leaving. The card asked which performance they enjoyed most and whether they would attend the event again. A volunteer counted the responses and reported the three most frequently selected performances at the final staff meeting.

# Vendor and Programme Notes

Each food vendor received a numbered pitch and a printed copy of the opening hours. The event secretary kept the vendor telephone numbers in a password-protected contact file so that schedule changes could be communicated. The file was deleted after outstanding invoices had been settled.

Photographs from the weekend were uploaded to the festival website. Captions listed the artist, the work, and the evening on which the photograph was taken. Website visit statistics were reviewed to decide which gallery page should appear first in the following year's promotional campaign.

The closing note thanked the musicians, artists, food vendors, ticket staff, and visitors. It recommended a larger performance tent and an additional pottery table for the next arts weekend.
