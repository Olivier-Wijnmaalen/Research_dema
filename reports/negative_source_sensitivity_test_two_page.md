# Sensitivity analysis: negative-source injection

See the formatted two-page Word document for the full thesis-ready results. Core findings: 41/155 controls were positively used in supporting-evidence fields, while 69/155 were separately referenced as contrary or limiting evidence. Positive control use affected 18/31 assessments. The mean phase changed from 2.77 to 1.42, although that comparison is descriptive because the experimental condition also removed most genuine baseline evidence.
